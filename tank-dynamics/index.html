    How to Play

    Your turn

    • Move ← → (or ◀ ▶) — uses fuel
    • Aim ↑ ↓ (or ▲ ▼) — angle is shown on the dial
    • Shoot hold SPACE (or FIRE) to charge power, let go to fire
    • Shots 1 · 2 · SS — the Super charges as you deal and take damage
    • 🔍 / Z see the whole map · ⏭ / Q pass

    Winning

    • Knock out every enemy tank, or sink them in the water.
    • Wind pushes every shot — check the arrow at the top!
    • The white ▼ on the power bar marks your last shot. Adjust from there.
    • Delay: big shots and long drives make you wait longer for your next turn.
    • Craters are real: blast the ground out from under a tank for a big fall.
    • After turn 26 it's Sudden Death: the water rises!
